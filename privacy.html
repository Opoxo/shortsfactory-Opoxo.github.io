Privacy Policy: Shorts Factory

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Shorts Factory is a private tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by a single person, its owner, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This page explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Factory uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): used only to upload videos to the connected channel and set their title, description, tags and scheduled publication time.
  • Read-only access (youtube.readonly): used to display the name of the connected channel and to read public information (title, publication date, duration, view count) about recent videos of specific source channels chosen by the owner.

No other Google data is accessed: no email, no contacts, no comments, no analytics.

Storage

Authorization tokens and the names of the connected channels are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's, and never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. The tool does not use advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while a channel stays connected. Disconnecting a channel in the tool deletes its stored token. Access can also be revoked at any time from your Google account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
