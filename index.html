Shorts Factory

A private publishing tool for my own YouTube channels.

Shorts Factory is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It translates and re-voices short videos (in English and French) that the owner has the rights to republish, then uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels and schedules their publication.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, nothing to sign up for, and it is used only by its owner. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channels and to read the list of recent videos of specific source channels.

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
